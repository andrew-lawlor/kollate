# Third-party data shipped with Kollate

## English Wiktionary

`/usr/share/kollate/dictionaries/wiktionary-en.db` (in the Flatpak,
`/app/share/kollate/dictionaries/`) is converted from the English
monolingual dictionary published by reader.dict (https://www.reader-dict.com/),
which is compiled from Wiktionary (https://www.wiktionary.org/).

© Wiktionary contributors. Licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The converted database is
distributed under the same licence.

Changes: converted from DictFile to Kollate's SQLite dictionary format;
etymologies, usage notes, synonym lists and quotations are left out.

"reader.dict" is a trademark of its owner. Kollate is not affiliated with
reader.dict or the Wikimedia Foundation.
